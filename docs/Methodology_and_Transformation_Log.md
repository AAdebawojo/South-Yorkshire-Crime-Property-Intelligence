## 3. Relationships (verified, Q2)

**Enforced foreign keys (8):** crimes→crime_categories, crimes→forces, crimes→locations (nullable), locations→neighbourhoods (nullable), neighbourhood_boundary_points→neighbourhoods (cascade), neighbourhoods→forces, outcomes→locations (nullable), outcomes→outcome_categories.

**Implied, not enforced:** outcomes.persistent_id → crimes.persistent_id (99.99% match; not unique in crimes: 4 duplicate pairs). outcomes.crime_category_code → crime_categories. crimes.crime_month → available_months. crimes.latest_outcome (name) ↔ outcome_categories.outcome_name.

**Cardinality:** crime → outcomes is 1 to 0..5; 99.35% of crimes with outcomes have exactly 1 outcome row.
